Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about procurement identity, verification, and submissions.

What is a Verifiable Credential (VC) in government tenders? ▼
A Verifiable Credential is a digitally signed file containing tender awards and details. It complies with W3C standards, meaning anyone can instantly check its authenticity cryptographically using the public ledger registry without relying on database searches.
How do I sign bids as a vendor? ▼
Vendors sign their bid submissions during enrollment by linking their eSignet identity profile. This verifies that the company details submitted match the government-held corporate registry records.
Are uploaded documents secure? ▼
Yes, all documents uploaded to your profile are encrypted at rest and tied to your digital key registry. Only verified government reviewers and authorized automated agents can decrypt them for tender validation.
